Our Courses

The Department of Data Technology and Analytics offers courses designed to provide students with both theoretical knowledge and practical skills. The courses introduce learners to important areas of computing, data management and information technology.

Students are exposed to different technologies used to collect, organise, store and analyse information. These skills are increasingly important because organisations depend on accurate data to make effective decisions.

The department also promotes practical learning through programming, database development, web design and data analysis activities. Students are encouraged to apply classroom knowledge to real-world problems.
