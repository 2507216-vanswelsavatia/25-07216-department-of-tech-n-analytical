Welcome to Our Department

Welcome to the Department of Data Technology and Analytics. Our department focuses on preparing students with the knowledge, skills and practical experience required to work in the modern technology and data-driven environment.

Data has become an important resource for organisations, businesses and institutions. Our students learn how data can be collected, stored, processed, analysed and transformed into useful information that supports decision-making.

Through practical learning, students are introduced to databases, data analysis, programming, web development and information systems. The department encourages students to combine theoretical knowledge with practical technological skills.

Our learning environment encourages creativity, innovation and problem-solving. Students are prepared to use technology responsibly and effectively in different professional environments.

What We Focus On

Data Technology

Learning how data is collected, stored, managed and processed using modern technologies.

Data Analytics

Developing skills for analysing data and producing useful information for decision-making.

Innovation

Encouraging students to develop creative technological solutions to real-world problems.
