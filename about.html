Who We Are

The Department of Data Technology and Analytics is an academic department focused on developing students' understanding of data, technology and digital information. The department provides an environment where students can develop both academic and practical skills.

In today's digital world, organisations generate large amounts of information every day. Our department helps students understand how this information can be collected, managed, analysed and used to support effective decisions.

Learning within the department combines classroom theory with practical activities. Students can develop skills in areas such as programming, databases, data analysis, information systems and web development.

We also encourage creativity, teamwork, critical thinking and problem-solving. These qualities help students prepare for further studies as well as careers in the technology and data industry.

Our Mission and Vision

Our Mission

To provide quality education and practical technological skills that prepare students to solve data and technology challenges.

Our Vision

To develop innovative and skilled professionals capable of using data and technology to create positive solutions in society.
